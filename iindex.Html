Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and understand workforce distribution, employee attrition, job roles, departments, demographics, and employee income. This dashboard helps HR teams understand employee trends and supports data-driven decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze total employee count.
  • Understand employee attrition.
  • Calculate employee attrition rate.
  • Analyze employee distribution by department.
  • Identify attrition by job role.
  • Analyze employee age distribution.
  • Compare attrition by gender.
  • Understand employee monthly income.
  • Analyze employee experience and retention.

Data Analysis
